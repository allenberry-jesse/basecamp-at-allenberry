Concierge health and wellness, built into where you live.

For too long there have been two options: stay in a house that was never built for the years ahead, or sell it and move into a community built around care. Basecamp is the space in between — a home you own, at a resort you love, with the healthcare you’d otherwise have to move for.

Basecamp is your home base — where every adventure begins and where you always feel at home. It’s an intergenerational neighborhood, inspired by the way families used to live, where elders stay at home and part of the community instead of moving away for care. Homes are designed so a parent or grown child can move in down the road. And as life changes, Basecamp is designed to change with you.

Care that comes to you.

This is what no other community does. Here, care isn’t a building you move into someday — it’s part of your neighborhood from the day you arrive. Better health starts with daily life: fresh air, trails at the door, yoga by the creek, healthy food, The Spa and neighbors of every generation.

Every home comes with a concierge medicine membership, so proactive, personal care is part of living here from the day you move in — with more support added to one care plan if you ever need it. And it isn’t only for later in life. Whether you’re managing a chronic condition, coming back from a sports injury or surgery, caring for someone you love or just want a physician who actually knows you, the same care team is here for you. If life ever asks for more, support is right here, so needing more never has to mean leaving.

World-class partners. Care close to home.

The Burick Center, PAM Health and Choice Health At Home work as one care team.

Concierge functional medicine

Burick Center for Health and Wellness

Burick brings 19 years of experience in concierge functional medicine to Basecamp: direct physician access, an annual comprehensive evaluation, longevity programming and an on-site nurse practitioner — with a focus on root causes rather than symptoms, helping residents stay healthy, active and independent.

Acute care & rehab

PAM Health

A national operator of long-term acute care and inpatient rehabilitation hospitals. PAM Health brings a nurse navigator, remote monitoring, and therapy and infusion at home or at its Hampden clinic. When something serious happens, residents have a priority pathway into PAM Health Rehabilitation Hospital of Hampden, with a warm hand-off from the Basecamp care team, hospital-level treatment and intensive rehab from specialists in complex recovery, and a smooth return home.

Home health & personal care

Choice Health At Home

Basecamp’s designated home care partner. Choice brings home health, personal care and twice-daily medication management to residents’ homes.

What concierge care really means.

Call your doctor and actually get through.

No more waiting weeks for an appointment or sitting on hold. Your physician knows you, you can reach them directly, and when you need a specialist, they help you get in faster.

A check-up that looks at the whole you.

A comprehensive whole-health evaluation every year — not a rushed visit about one symptom, but a full picture of how you’re doing.

A plan to stay well, not just get well.

Longevity programming built around your goals, from strength and mobility to sleep and nutrition.

More support, only if you need it.

Check-ups that come to you.

A nurse practitioner stops by the house to check your blood pressure, review your medications and see how you’re doing.

Same-day care when something feels off.

Instead of an urgent care waiting room, you get seen the same day, often without leaving home.

A PAM Health nurse on call.

A PAM Health nurse navigator is there to answer questions, coordinate your care and help you see the right specialist sooner.

Recover in your own living room.

After a stroke, surgery or fall, you get a priority path into PAM Health Rehabilitation Hospital of Hampden, with no waiting through the usual placement process. Then it’s back home, where physical, occupational and speech therapy, home health and your nurse practitioner see you through recovery.

Hyperbaric therapy, right on site.

A hyperbaric oxygen chamber here at The Spa at Allenberry, so therapy your care team recommends is a short walk from home.

Help at home, on your terms.

Personal care and medication management from Choice Health At Home, for as much or as little as you need.

Never think about laundry again.

Housekeeping and laundry from the Allenberry team, so your time goes to the things you love.

Not an amenities package. Your home.

Other communities build a clubhouse and call it a lifestyle. Here, a historic resort is your backyard — The Playhouse, The Spa, the creek and the restaurants are steps from your front door. You don’t go to the resort. You come home to it.

The Playhouse

A 250-seat historic theater on site, open since 1949 — concerts, film, comedy and live performance. Dinner at the Barn before the show, then a short walk home.

Fly fishing

A mile of catch-and-release on the Yellow Breeches, one of Pennsylvania’s celebrated limestone trout streams. An Orvis-endorsed lodge, with local guides and lessons for first-time casters.

Fitness & classes

Movement studio, strength room and group classes on the schedule — plus yoga by the creek, and the Appalachian Trail just outside your door.

The Spa

Eight treatment rooms, open year-round — massage, facials, and daily wellness sessions from red light therapy to infrared sauna.

Dining

The Barn, a seasonal American restaurant in a late-1700s barn; morning coffee at the Breeches Café; summer evenings on the Terrace above the creek. Menus built around local farms.

Creekside gathering

Shared meals, lawn concerts and the everyday chance to run into a neighbor — the easy, unplanned moments that turn a place into a community.
