Allenberry, reimagined

Everything you come here for is already here.

For generations, Allenberry has been where people come to get away — for a show, a weekend, a wedding, a day on the creek. It’s a historic resort that keeps growing and evolving, and Basecamp is its next chapter: a multigenerational community of thoughtfully designed homes, woven right into the resort grounds.

Basecamp asks you to see the place you know differently: not as somewhere you visit, or even just somewhere you live, but as somewhere that becomes part of your story. Imagine the trail starting at your door, a concert at the Playhouse, and dinner at the Barn a short walk home.

A resort community where wellness, nature and the people around you are part of everyday life — and where neighbors become an extension of your family. The people who wave from the trail, save you a seat at the Playhouse and check in when you’ve been away.

It’s also a better answer to a question more and more families face: where do we live as we grow older? Conventional senior living is built around decline, and asks you to leave your home and your community behind. Basecamp is built around living well, every day and at every age — a real home, in a place you love, surrounded by every generation, with wellness woven into daily life and support close by if you ever need it.

Why Basecamp

Your home base, for every adventure.

Basecamp is where every adventure begins and where you always feel at home — a place to explore, reconnect, recharge and experience everything life has to offer. And as life changes, Basecamp is designed to change with you.

Less upkeep. More life.

We take care of the landscaping and exterior maintenance, so your time goes to what you came for — the trail, the creek, the people, and everything the resort has to offer. And when life asks more of you, that same freedom means you can focus on getting better, or on caring for the people you love.

Is it for you? Basecamp is for every generation — anyone who…

  • is raising a young family and wants kids to grow up by the creek, with grandparents close.
  • is starting a new chapter and wants to be part of a community that means something.
  • would rather spend weekends on the trail than behind a mower.
  • wants generations under one roof — a suite for a parent or an adult child, with privacy for everyone.
  • is ready to downsize from the family house without downsizing their life.
  • wants to age in place, surrounded by every generation.

Wellness & care

Live well in every season of life.

Wellness here isn’t something you turn to when you’re sick — it’s part of every day: a walk on the trail, yoga by the creek, the Spa, good food and good neighbors. And if life ever asks for more, support is right here, so needing more never has to mean leaving.

The residences

A home designed to evolve with you.

The resort is the setting. The homes are what make Basecamp different. Homes are planned with an integrated suite and an elevator, so the same home can work for a young family today, a parent moving in down the road, and a lifetime of living well after that.

Architecture

Modern, in the trees

Clean-lined homes in natural materials, with big windows, light-filled rooms and views into the woods — designed to sit lightly on the land.

Integrated suites

A suite of their own

Homes include a private, self-contained suite for a parent, a grown child, a caregiver or guests — close by, with a door of its own.

Elevators

Every floor, for life

Elevator access in every home, with accessibility designed in from the start rather than added later.

Flexible living

Rooms that change with you

Layouts that adapt as households grow, shrink and change — an office becomes a nursery, a guest room becomes a suite.

Ownership

Primary or second home

Make Basecamp your everyday address, or the second home you can't wait to get back to for weekends, holidays and summers on the creek.

Rental program

Earn while you're away

Owners may choose to join an Allenberry-managed rental program and earn income when they're not using their home, subject to program terms.

The resort is home

Not an amenities package. Your home.

Other communities sell you a clubhouse. At Basecamp there's nothing to buy into and nowhere to visit — the Playhouse, the Spa, the trails and the restaurants are simply part of where you live. You don't go to the resort. You come home to it. And through the Allenberry Institute for Learning and Renewal, there's always something to learn — lectures, retreats and workshops in wellness, nature and the arts.

The Playhouse

A 250-seat historic theater on site, open since 1949 — concerts, film, comedy and live performance. Dinner at the Barn before the show, then a short walk home.

Fly fishing

A mile of catch-and-release on the Yellow Breeches, one of Pennsylvania’s celebrated limestone trout streams. An Orvis-endorsed lodge, with local guides and lessons for first-time casters.

Fitness & classes

Movement studio, strength room and group classes on the schedule — plus yoga by the creek, and the Appalachian Trail just outside your door.

The Spa

Eight treatment rooms, open year-round — massage, facials, and daily wellness sessions from red light therapy to infrared sauna.

Dining

The Barn, a seasonal American restaurant in a late-1700s barn; morning coffee at the Breeches Café; summer evenings on the Terrace above the creek. Menus built around local farms.

Creekside gathering

Shared meals, lawn concerts and the everyday chance to run into a neighbor — the easy, unplanned moments that turn a place into a community.
