Care at Basecamp

Support for every season of life.

Whether you're staying well day to day, recovering from an injury or surgery, or caring for someone you love, support is built into the community — so needing more never has to mean leaving.

Our approach

Wellness first. Support when you want it.

At Basecamp, care isn't a package you buy into or a wing you move to. It's a set of options woven into the community — there when you want them, out of the way when you don't. Most days, wellness here looks like a walk on the trail, yoga by the creek or an afternoon at the Spa. When life asks for more, help is close by.

Stay well

Everyday, proactive wellness that keeps you doing what you love — on the trail, on the creek and at the Spa.

Choose what you need

Every option is just that: optional. Use as much or as little as you like, and change your mind anytime.

Stay close

Support right here at home, surrounded by family, neighbors and the place you love.

Care options

Three ways we've got you.

Concierge medicine

Daily Health Shield

An on-site functional-medicine concierge practice focused on keeping you well day to day — not just treating you when something goes wrong. It's care that gets to know you, looks at the whole picture, and works hand in hand with the trail, the Spa and the kitchen at home.

  • Unhurried appointments with a practitioner who knows you
  • Personalized plans for movement, nutrition, sleep and stress
  • A whole-person, root-cause approach to everyday health
  • Coordination with your own doctors and specialists

Aging in place

Care on campus

Health-care students are available for basic care on site, à la carte — so you can grow older without growing apart, with help only when you want it. Stay in your own home, in the community you love, with a little extra support close by.

  • Basic care, booked only when you need it
  • Regular wellness check-ins
  • A helping hand getting around campus
  • Support that grows with you, at your own pace

Respite & recovery

Recovery suites

After an injury or surgery, stop here along the way on your road to recovery. Our rental suites offer a comfortable, light-filled place for rehab and respite care, close to family and home. These suites are rented, not sold.

  • Short-term stays for rehab after surgery or injury
  • Respite stays that give family caregivers a break
  • A smooth handoff from the hospital back to home
  • Fresh air, creek walks and the Spa as part of getting better

The acute safety net

Priority care when it matters, and a clear path home.

For advanced or complex medical needs, or sudden rehabilitation, Basecamp has an expedited alliance with a leading provider of long-term acute care and inpatient rehabilitation hospitals. Instead of waiting through the usual placement process, residents have a direct, priority path to specialized care — and back home again.

Expedited placement

If an illness, stroke or orthopedic event happens, residents get priority placement into a partner long-term acute care or inpatient rehabilitation hospital.

Specialized care

Hospital-level treatment and intensive rehabilitation from a team that focuses on complex recovery.

Recovery at home

Once stabilized, residents transition smoothly back to Basecamp, where the on-site concierge team and nursing students manage post-acute recovery.

For families

Caring for someone you love, without carrying it alone.

Basecamp was shaped by the experience of caring for aging parents. Here, the people you love can be close by — in an integrated suite in your home, with an elevator to every floor — and well looked after — while you keep your own life, your own time and your own peace of mind.

A suite of their own

A parent can live in a private suite in your home — close enough for Sunday dinner, with a door of their own.

A break when you need it

Respite stays in a recovery suite give caregivers time to rest, travel or simply breathe.

Help that fits

Basic care on campus, booked when it's needed, so family can be family first.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Basecamp a senior living community?+

No. Basecamp is a multigenerational resort community. Care options are there for anyone who wants them, at any age — and you never have to use them.

Do I have to sign up for care services?+

No. Every service is optional. Use as much or as little as you like, whenever you like.

What if I need more care than Basecamp offers?+

Through our expedited alliance with a leading post-acute care provider, residents with advanced or complex medical needs get priority placement in a partner long-term acute care or inpatient rehabilitation hospital — then a smooth transition back home to recover.
